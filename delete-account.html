Account & data deletion

You can delete your AgriView account from inside the app, or by submitting a request here. When deletion is completed, we delete or anonymize the account data we're not legally or legitimately required to retain — see our Privacy Policy for full details.

  1. 1
    In the app: go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, confirm, and your request is submitted instantly.
  2. 2
    From this page: fill in the form below. It opens your email app with a pre-filled deletion request addressed to our support team.
  3. 3
    What gets deleted: your name/username, email, phone number, account identifiers, scan history and results, quiz/MCQ progress, bookmarks and favorites, and other account-associated data — except what we're required to retain for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.
This action is permanent. Once your account deletion is processed, it cannot be undone.
