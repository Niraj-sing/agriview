The field guide

Everything an agriculture student or farmer reaches for, catalogued in one place.

Ten features, indexed like specimens in a field guide — so you always know exactly where to look.

AG·01 CROP

Crop Package of Practices

Complete, crop-wise package of practices from sowing to harvest — season, spacing, seed rate, fertiliser schedule and irrigation, ready for quick revision or field reference.

AG·02 PYQ

PYQ Bank — every major exam

Previous year questions for NABARD, AFO, State PCS (Agriculture) and ICAR JRF across all subjects, plus chapter-wise practice sets for focused revision.

AG·03 NOTE

University notes — ANRAU, ICAR, TNAU

Curated study notes sourced from ANRAU, ICAR and TNAU, organised subject-wise so you can revise from trusted material without hunting across the internet.

AG·04 SCAN

Smart Crop Scan (AI)

Point your camera at a crop and AgriView returns the crop and scientific name, flags pest or disease, and lays out prevention, biological control and chemical control — in one scan.

AG·05 PEST

Pest library — chapter-wise

Browse pests organised exactly the way your syllabus is, chapter by chapter, so exam-day revision takes minutes, not hours.

AG·06 DISEASE

Disease library — crop-wise

Look up diseases crop by crop, with symptoms and management laid out for quick field diagnosis or exam revision.

AG·07 VARIETY

Variety directory (up to 2026)

Around 128 crops covered, with released varieties tracked up to 2026 and organised crop-wise, so you always know what's current.

AG·08 NUTRIENT

Nutrient deficiency & toxicity guide

Deficiency and toxicity symptoms for every major nutrient, paired with crop images for pests and diseases so you can identify problems by sight.

AG·09 WEATHER

Real-time weather

Location-based, real-time weather right inside the app, so field decisions are never made blind.

AG·10 JOBS

Agriculture job alerts

Real-time notifications for agriculture jobs and recruitment, so relevant openings never pass you by.

Flagship feature

Smart Scan: point, and know.

A single photo takes you from "what is wrong with this crop" to a full diagnosis and control plan.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Take a photo of the crop, leaf, or affected part directly in the app.

  2. 2

    Identify

    AgriView identifies the crop and its scientific name, and flags any pest or disease present.

  3. 3

    Act

    Get prevention steps, biological control and chemical control options — the full picture, in one place.

About AgriView

Built by a student, for students.

NK

Niraj Kumar

Founder & Creator, AgriView · Student, ICAR‑IARI

AgriView is built and maintained by Niraj Kumar, a student at ICAR‑IARI (Indian Agricultural Research Institute). It grew out of a simple frustration: agriculture students and farmers had to jump between a dozen scattered sources for crop information, exam preparation and field diagnosis. AgriView brings all of it into one free app.
